Engineering industrial temperature solutions since 1986.

Our Legacy of Precision

Yog Electro Process Private Limited has served industry since 1986 with a focus on accurate temperature measurement, dependable process control, industrial heating, research and customer-specific engineering.

Today, we manufacture Thermocouples, RTDs, Thermowells and complementary instrumentation. Our commitment to accuracy is reinforced by our in-house ISO/IEC 17025:2017 NABL accredited calibration laboratory.

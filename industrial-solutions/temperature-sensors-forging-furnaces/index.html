Temperature Sensors for Forging Furnaces

Forging furnace temperature sensors support measurement around billet heating, furnace zones, dies and related heat-treatment operations. The correct choice follows the point and handling conditions.

Describe whether the sensor is for a billet-heating zone, furnace control, die or another identified point. Include the thermal cycle, atmosphere, scale, vibration, access, expected response and protection arrangement. Charge movement and repeated loading can make location and mounting as important as thermocouple type.

Thermocouple MI and R Type Thermocouple are identified options for forging-furnace work. Surface temperature assemblies can be relevant to localized contact points, and the catalogue also includes an Infrared Temperature Gun for non-contact instrument use. Each option addresses a different measurement method and should be matched to the point.

Confirm the required type, junction, sheath or protection, dimensions, cable and mounting from the product record. For a furnace zone, explain how the tip is positioned and replaced. For a billet or die surface, describe contact access and the desired reading location rather than relying on a general forging term.

Send the furnace drawing or point schedule, process conditions and instrument input with your enquiry. Yog Electro Process can discuss the listed thermocouple and temperature-measurement options against the actual forging setup.

Forging enquiries benefit from a point-by-point description of billet, furnace and tooling measurements. Ask whether contact is continuous or intermittent, whether the sensor is exposed to handling, and how the reading is transferred to the indicator or controller. Include the required cable route and the space available for protection. This separates a furnace thermocouple discussion from a surface or portable measurement discussion and makes the catalogue options easier to compare.

Include the furnace point drawing and the instrument channel in the enquiry. State whether the measurement is continuous, periodic or portable, and identify any access or cable-routing constraint. This gives the selection discussion a clear basis and keeps each proposed product connected to the forging point it is intended to measure.

State the desired reading unit, channel arrangement and connector standard where known. Clarify whether the assembly is changed between heats or remains installed. This helps keep the selected option aligned with the furnace method, wiring and maintenance routine.

Include the project point list, drawing references and instrument details so the buyer and engineering team discuss the same measurement arrangement.

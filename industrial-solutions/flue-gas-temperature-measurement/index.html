Flue Gas Temperature Measurement

Flue gas temperature measurement is a point-specific task in boiler and power systems. Furnace, uptake and flue paths can differ in access, atmosphere, heat and mounting.

Describe the flue-gas point, its purpose and the location in the gas path. Include normal and upset conditions, access, flow context, insertion, vibration, ash or other stated surroundings, cable route and receiving instrument. Distinguish control, monitoring and equipment-protection points when preparing the point list.

Thermocouple MI is listed for furnace, oven and heat-treatment measurement, and the catalogue includes head-type thermocouple records and a Two-Wire Loop-Power Temperature Transmitter for Field Mounting. The boiler and power application pages provide the relevant measurement context; the selected product record supplies the available construction.

Confirm thermocouple type, junction, sheath, dimensions, cable, head and transmitter interface. Do not infer a flue-gas range, pressure, material or installation detail from the page topic. If a protection or connection is required, describe it as a project requirement for review rather than assuming a catalogue option.

Share the gas-path sketch, point conditions, mounting and signal details with Yog Electro Process. This gives the team a clear basis for discussing the listed thermocouple and transmitter records for the intended flue-gas point.

Flue-gas enquiries should identify the gas-path location, the reason for the reading and the available mounting arrangement. Ask about insertion, access, vibration, ash or other stated surroundings, cable route and the signal expected by the instrument. If a transmitter is needed, include the field connection and loop requirement. A point schedule with these details helps separate an MI thermocouple discussion from a head-type assembly or field transmitter discussion.

Include the boiler or flue-path point schedule and instrument details. Identify the intended mounting location and access for replacement. This keeps the catalogue discussion focused on the stated gas-path measurement and makes the requested thermocouple or transmitter interface clear to the buyer.

State the desired reading unit, channel arrangement and connector standard where known. Clarify whether the assembly remains installed or is changed during boiler service. This helps keep the selected option aligned with the flue-gas method, wiring and maintenance routine.

Include the project point list, drawing references and instrument details so the buyer and engineering team discuss the same measurement arrangement.

Before discussing a sensor, state the gas-path point, mounting arrangement and signal in the purchase brief. Include any drawing reference and identify whether the assembly is intended for ongoing monitoring or periodic replacement. This keeps the request specific to the documented flue-gas measurement and the catalogue records under consideration.

Humidity Temperature Transmitter

Humidity and temperature measurement play an important role in any process industry. A Humidity Temperature Transmitter is constructed using a smart sensor for humidity measurement and a highly precise Pt-100 for temperature measurement. The input signal generated by the transducer is fed to a signal conditioning circuit. The linearization of the input is performed by the linearization circuit. The output signal (4-20 mA) is achieved through a highly accurate and reliable output circuit.

Thermocouple – Transition Type · WP-77

Jubilee-Clip Thermocouple for Pipe-Surface Measurement

A thermocouple is employed to measure the surface temperature of pipeline systems that carry water, gas, chemicals, or oil in various industries such as chemical, food processing, dairy, breweries, and refineries. To ensure a secure and sturdy mounting system, a Jubilee clip is utilized. For corrosive atmospheres, an SS clip can be provided as an alternative.
